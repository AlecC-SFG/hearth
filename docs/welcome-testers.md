# Welcome, Hearth testers!

Thanks for helping test **Hearth**, a calm task app built for families, especially
anyone who gets stuck choosing what to do, gets lost in one thing for hours, or
starts ten things and finishes none.

## Get started (one person per family)

1. On your phone, open **https://alecc-sfg.github.io/hearth/**
   - iPhone: use **Safari**, tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
   - Android: use **Chrome**, tap **⋮** → **Install app**.
2. Open Hearth from your home screen and choose **Set up my family**.
3. Add everyone's names and leave **Share with everyone's phones** on.
4. Go to **Settings** (gear icon) and tap **Send invite**. Text it to your family,
   or let them scan the QR code with their phone camera.

## Everyone else in the family

Open the invite link (or the app, then **Join my family** and type the code), install
it to your home screen the same way, and pick your name.

## Things to try

- **Now** tab: it shows just one thing to do. Try *Start*, *It's done*, and *Not this one*.
- Add a few real chores and assign them to different people.
- Start a focus timer and let it run out. What happens?
- Try starting a third task while two are already in progress.
- Check a chore off on one phone and watch it update on another.

## Tell us what you think

Tap **Send feedback** (in Settings, or the side menu on a computer). Anything helps:
bugs, confusing bits, ideas, or the parts you like.

## Privacy

Your family's lists are shared only among phones that joined with your code. Other
families testing Hearth can't see them. Lists are stored on Hearth's test server
so your phones can sync, and the person running the test can access that server
for troubleshooting. Please don't put passwords, bank details, or anything sensitive
in your tasks.
